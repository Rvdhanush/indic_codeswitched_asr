01 — The failure

It doesn't mistranscribe.
It collapses.

Hit Whisper-small with an English word mid-Tamil-sentence and it does not return a wrong English word. The decoder loses confidence, locks onto a high-probability local pattern, and repeats it — here, one Tamil word, twenty-five times.

Whisper-small · baseline

Repetition collapse

No hallucination. Tamil is correct. English is heard correctly but rendered in Tamil script — imperfect, and recoverable.

03 — Taxonomy

Errors get categorised, not counted

A WER number tells you a model is failing. It doesn't tell you what to do next. Every error is sorted into one of five categories — and the two that dominate are the same two across all three baselines.

Systemic · top-2 for every baseline

SUBSTITUTION_SWITCH

The transcription breaks at the boundary where the language changes.

Systemic · top-2 for every baseline

LANGUAGE_CONFUSION

The word is heard correctly and written in the wrong script.

Category 03

DELETION_PROPER_NOUN

A named entity is dropped from the output entirely.

Category 04

SUBSTITUTION_NUMBER

A number, date, or digit sequence comes out wrong.

Category 05

INSERTION_FILLER

A filler word appears that nobody said.

The same two failures top every architecture. That makes them blind spots in seq2seq decoding, not bugs in any one model.

04 — Method

The taxonomy picks the training data

Public Tamil–English corpora are gated or unusable, so the training set is synthesised — which means every switch point is known ground truth rather than a guess.

Step 01

Build the corpus

MUCS 2021 needs an academic agreement. IndicVoices writes English loanwords in Tamil script, destroying the language labels. So: Tamil from IndicVoices-R joined to English from LibriSpeech.

Sample rate16 kHz
Segment length2–8 s
Split80 / 10 / 10
Step 02

Weight what fails

The sampler is the failure analysis translated into a data strategy. Segments with many switch points — the ones the taxonomy flagged — are seen six times as often as they occur.

Code-switched×3
High switch-point×6
Monolingual×0.5
Step 03

Adapt, don't retrain

Full fine-tuning on this much data would trade away the base model's general speech ability. LoRA touches the attention projections and leaves the encoder and language head frozen.

Two billion people code-switch.
No ASR system expects it.

Tamil is one language pair. The method — categorise the failures, then weight the training data by category — carries to Hinglish, Telugu, Kannada, and anywhere else two languages share a sentence.
